# Privacy Policy — Clock+

**App:** Clock+ (`com.mkm7dev.clock`)
**Last updated:** 16 October 2026

Clock+ is an offline clock and alarm app for Android. This policy explains what the app does and
does not do with your information.

> **In short:** Clock+ works entirely on your device. It contains no advertising, no analytics and
> no tracking, and it does not collect, transmit or share your personal data. The only optional
> network feature is the voluntary "Support" tip, which is processed by Google Play (see §5).

## 1. Information we collect

**None.** Clock+ has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no advertising, analytics or
crash-reporting services. The app's own code makes no network requests. We operate no servers and
receive no data from the app.

## 2. Data stored on your device

Everything you create stays in the app's private storage on your device, including:

- your alarms and alarm groups;
- app settings and preferences (theme, sounds, reminders, snooze length, and similar);
- the cities you add to the World Clock;
- "don't show again" flags for optional permission prompts.

This data is used only to run the app for you. It is never sent to us or to any third party, and it
is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android Settings.

## 3. Android backup

If Android's device backup is enabled on your phone, your alarms and settings may be included in
your own Google account backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings;
we have no access to it. You can turn it off in **Settings → System → Backup**.

## 4. Permissions we request

Clock+ asks only for what a reliable alarm clock needs, and the optional ones can be declined:

- **Notifications** — to show alarms, timers and reminders.
- **Exact alarms** — so alarms ring at the time you set (Android 12+).
- **Full-screen notifications** — to show the alarm screen when it rings (Android 14+).
- **Vibration** — for alarm and timer vibration.
- **Wake the screen / show over the lock screen** — to display the ringing alarm.
- **Run at startup** — to reschedule your alarms after the phone restarts.
- **Foreground service (media playback)** — to play the alarm sound reliably while ringing.
- **Ignore battery optimizations** *(optional)* — so your phone does not delay alarms.
- **Do Not Disturb access** *(optional)* — so the app appears in your DND settings.
- **Read audio files** *(optional)* — only if you pick a custom alarm sound from your device.

## 5. In-app purchases (Support tips)

Clock+ offers **optional, voluntary tips** under a "Support" section, using **Google Play Billing**.
Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We do **not** receive your name, card details or
any payment information. Google may report an anonymous purchase confirmation to the app so it can
thank you and let you give the same tip again. Tipping unlocks **nothing** — no feature or content
is gated behind it. Google's handling of purchase data is governed by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 6. Third-party components

The only third-party component in the app is **Google Play Billing** (Google LLC), used solely for
the optional tips in §5. It adds the Android `INTERNET` and network-state permissions, which the
app's own code does not use. No data is sold or shared with anyone.

## 7. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information, because we never receive any.

## 8. Data retention and deletion

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. To remove all app data
from your device, uninstall Clock+ or use **Android Settings → Apps → Clock+ → Storage → Clear
data**. Any copy in an Android backup is controlled by your own Google account (see §3).

## 9. Children's privacy

Clock+ is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly
collect information from children — indeed, we collect no information from anyone.

## 10. Security

Your data lives in the app's private, sandboxed storage, protected by the Android platform. Because
the app transmits nothing, there is no data in transit to protect.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the "Last updated" date above will change. Continued
use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
